My Life Dashboard · Personal workspace

Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

My Life Dashboard is a privately administered, approval-based personal organization website. This policy describes how the dashboard handles information, including information obtained through Google APIs.

Information we process

  • Google sign-in information, including your account identifier, email address and verified sign-in status, to authenticate you and administer access.
  • Access requests and their approval status.
  • Information you choose to enter, such as financial transactions, academic records, work tasks, vehicle records, notes, workspace content and preferences.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, calendar identifiers, names, calendar details and event information needed to display and manage your calendar.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the dashboard's features, synchronize your saved dashboard data across devices, administer access, maintain security and respond to support or deletion requests. Google Calendar access is optional. The dashboard reads calendar and event information to display your calendar and uses event permissions to create, edit or delete events when you choose those actions. It does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, profiling, sale or training AI models.

Google API data

My Life Dashboard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is requested directly from Google to provide calendar features; the dashboard does not intentionally persist copies of fetched Google Calendar events in its Firestore database. Calendar authorization tokens may be retained in your browser's local or session storage until they expire or are cleared. You can disconnect Calendar in the dashboard and revoke access in your Google Account connections.

Storage, access and service providers

Dashboard records and access requests are stored using Google Firebase/Cloud Firestore. GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Google provides authentication and Calendar services. These providers may process information as necessary to operate their services, including technical request information. Firebase security rules are intended to isolate each approved user's dashboard records. The project administrator manages access and can technically access stored dashboard records through administrative tools. Other approved users are not intended to have access to your records. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Saved dashboard records are retained until deleted. Revoking dashboard access preserves saved information unless it is separately deleted. You may export your dashboard data while authorized, delete your own dashboard data through Settings, or contact the administrator to request deletion of your stored dashboard data and access records. Deletion from the dashboard does not delete events held in your Google Calendar, your own exported files or copies already present on your devices. Service-provider retention or backup processes may apply. Sign out and clear local site data on shared devices.

Security

Google authentication, encrypted HTTPS connections and Firebase access rules help protect information. No online service is risk-free. Do not store passwords, authentication secrets or other highly sensitive information in the dashboard; it is not a dedicated encrypted password manager.

Contact and changes

For questions, privacy requests or deletion requests, contact the dashboard administrator at ggparise100@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
